Zakon is an arcade flying game taken in place in country called Krasnogorie. You, as the player, will take a role of a futurustic police jet. You will be tasked with protecting Rostogrvad, the capital of Krasnogorie from inside and outside enemies.

During the gameplay you will have to fly through the incredibly urbanistic city while patroling the citizines. Make sure that they are nothing suspecuos going on in their minds.

You will be working with Katya, your communication manager. She will monitor your actions

However, citizines is not the only problem to deal with. Our enemies are attacking us yet again. Let's see whose technologies will win.

Glory to krasnogorie! Slava Vozhdu!
